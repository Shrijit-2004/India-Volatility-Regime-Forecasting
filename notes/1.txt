## GARCH vs EGARCH — Nifty 50 (2015–2026)

- GARCH(1,1): alpha=0.104, beta=0.871 (alpha+beta=0.975 → high persistence)
- EGARCH(1,1): alpha=0.166, gamma=-0.097 (p<0.001), beta=0.967
- EGARCH AIC (7219.67) < GARCH AIC (7285.59) → EGARCH fits significantly better
- Negative, significant gamma confirms the LEVERAGE EFFECT: negative shocks
  increase future volatility more than positive shocks of equal size.
- Conclusion: Nifty 50 volatility is asymmetric; EGARCH is the preferred
  classical benchmark model going forward.
